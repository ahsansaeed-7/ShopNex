SHOPNEX SUPPORT

Returns & Exchanges

We want you to be happy with your ShopNex purchase. If you need to return an item, please contact our support team.

Return Requirements

  • The product should be unused and in its original condition.
  • Original packaging should be retained where possible.
  • Contact ShopNex before sending a product back.
  • Certain products may not be eligible for return.

How to Request a Return

Contact us through the ShopNex Contact page and provide your order number and the reason for the return.

Refunds

Approved refunds are processed according to the payment method and applicable ShopNex return conditions.
