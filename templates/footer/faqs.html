SHOPNEX SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some of the most common questions about shopping with ShopNex.

How do I place an order?
Browse the products, add the items you want to your cart and continue to checkout. Complete your delivery information and place your order.
How can I track my order?
Open your My Orders section and select the order you want to track.
Can I return a product?
Yes, eligible products can be returned according to our Returns Policy.
How long does delivery take?
Delivery time depends on your location, product availability and shipping method.
What payment methods do you accept?
Available payment methods are displayed during checkout.
How can I contact ShopNex?
Visit our Contact Us page and send us a message. Our team will get back to you.
How can I subscribe to the newsletter?
Enter your email address in the newsletter section at the bottom of the website and click Subscribe.
